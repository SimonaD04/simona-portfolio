PÁLENVO — Client Project

Packaging Design · Label Design · Custom Illustration

Adobe Illustrator

Concept & Objective: This project was created for a Slovak client living in Switzerland who wanted a label for his homemade pálenka. The idea was to include something personal and meaningful to him, so I used the entrance gate of his grandfather's house in Bački Petrovac as the main illustration. I wanted the design to reflect his family history and connection to his hometown, while also making the product feel unique.

Zvonko Bogdan — Academic Project

Brand Identity · Packaging · Logo Design

Adobe Illustrator

Concept & Objective: This was a student project focused on creating a wine label inspired by the Zvonko Bogdan brand and its connection to the local wine and music culture. I wanted to combine two recognisable elements of the region: vineyards and tamburica music. The main illustration brings these elements together, giving the label a personal touch while keeping the overall design simple and elegant.

Fabrizia Limoncello Spritz — Academic Project

Brand Identity · Packaging · Logo Design

Adobe Illustrator

Concept & Objective: For this assignment, I had the freedom to choose any canned drink and create a new visual identity for it. I chose Fabrizia Limoncello Spritz because I liked the idea of working with a drink associated with summer and Italian culture. Foto Benka

Print Design · Design for Photo Studio · Commercial Project

Concept & Objective: This project involved creating different merchandise designs for Foto Benka, including T-shirts, tote bags and mugs. I wanted to make the products more personal by including elements of Slovak culture and local traditions. Web Design · Development

Figma, Wordpress
